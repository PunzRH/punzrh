How __SYM__ works

A meme coin whose trading fees short PONS: in an on-chain perp vault (v1) and in a real position on Robinhood Lighter held by a contract (v2). Both reprice every minute and any holder can redeem their share. Everything below is verifiable; addresses are at the bottom.

1. The idea

On 6 Sep 2026 @loraclexyz tweeted: "we need tokenized perp position on robinhood lighter, imagine pairing a meme coin with a PONS short." __SYM__ is that, built the only way it can be done without trusting anyone: the perp itself is a contract on Robinhood Chain, its positions are ERC‑20 tokens, and the meme coin's fees buy the short side automatically.

What makes it new. The short is never in a wallet a person controls. __SYM__ runs two shorts side by side, fed by the same fees: v1, a perp vault that lives entirely on Robinhood Chain (sections 3–6), and v2, a real PONS short on the Robinhood Lighter order book held by a contract (section 7). Both have no operator, and every holder can cash out their share of either, any time, by burning __SYM__.

2. What a perp short actually is

A perpetual short never sells the asset. It's a contract that pays you when the price falls and charges you when it rises, settled against an index price, with the other side of the trade as your counterparty. On Lighter or Hyperliquid the counterparty is an order book. In __SYM__'s vault the counterparty is whoever holds the long side of the same pool. Same instrument, different venue.

3. The vault: one pot of ETH, two sides

PONS price (2‑min TWAP from PONS's own Uniswap pool) │ ▼ every 30 s: rebalance() ┌───────────────┐ PONS ↑ : shorts pay longs ┌────────────────┐ │ LONG side │ ◄──────────────────────────── │ SHORT side │ │ (ETH) lPONS │ ────────────────────────────► │ (ETH) sPONS │ └───────────────┘ PONS ↓ : longs pay shorts └────────────────┘

The vault (PonsPerpPool) holds ETH in two balances, LONG and SHORT. Shares of each balance are ERC‑20 tokens: lPONS and sPONS. Every 30 seconds anyone can call rebalance(). It reads a 2‑minute time‑weighted average of PONS's price from PONS's own Uniswap v3 pool and moves ETH from the side that lost to the side that won:

  • PONS up x% → the SHORT balance pays x% of itself to LONG.
  • PONS down x% → the LONG balance pays x% of itself to SHORT.

NAV of a share = side balance ÷ share supply. That's the price at which anyone mints or burns. Nothing is borrowed, nothing is liquidated, there is no funding rate and no operator; the only input is PONS's own market price.

Why a TWAP and a two‑step mint

A spot price can be wicked for one block. The 2‑minute average can't be moved cheaply. And because the average lags, minting at "today's" NAV while knowing where the price is heading would be free money, so mints and burns are two‑step: commitMint now, claim at the first rebalance after a full 2‑minute window has passed. You get the NAV of the future, not the past.

The skew, honestly

Because the losing side pays out of its own balance, the payoff is not always 1:1. If the short side is 5× the long side, a 1% PONS drop pays shorts only 0.2% (all the longs had), while a 1% PONS rise costs shorts the full 1%. The mirror is true for longs: they get ~5% per 1% up, lose 1% per 1% down. Capital on the thin side fixes it, and the thin side is where the deal is. Take it.

4. __SYM__: the coin that buys the short

trader ──ETH──► __SYM__ pool (Uniswap v4, locked LP, 3% fee) │ ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐ ETH fee (buys) __SYM__ fee (sells) │ │ ▼ harvest(), anyone, ~1/min ▼ Backing contract ──► sPONS 🔥 burned │ ├─ if sPONS market ≤ NAV+3% : buy sPONS on the market └─ else : commitMint(SHORT) at NAV in the vault

__SYM__ is a plain ERC‑20 with no owner and a fixed 1 billion supply. It trades on a hookless Uniswap v4 pool quoted in ETH. The liquidity is a single‑sided locked position: the launcher contract's only owner function is collectFees(), and its owner is the Backing contract. Nobody can pull the liquidity.

Uniswap collects the 3% fee in whatever the trader paid with. Buys pay ETH, so buy fees become sPONS. Sells pay __SYM__, so sell fees are burned. harvest() on Backing does both, and anyone can call it; a keeper does, roughly once a minute.

5. Backing and the floor

The sPONS bought with fees sits in the Backing contract. It has no owner and no withdraw function. The only way sPONS leaves is __SYM__.redeem(amount): burn __SYM__, receive your pro‑rata share of the sPONS, instantly. That share is the floor under every __SYM__. It started at zero at launch, rises with every buy (more sPONS in), every sell (fewer __SYM__ to share it), and every PONS drop (each sPONS worth more ETH). It cannot be taken out from under holders because there is no function that does that.

Redeemed sPONS can be burned at the vault for ETH at NAV (two‑step, ~2 minutes) or sold on the sPONS/ETH market.

6. The peg bot, and why it pays holders

sPONS has a small public market (sPONS/ETH) so terminals can chart it and so Backing can buy instantly. Fees push that market above NAV; a keeper sells sPONS back down, keeping the chart at NAV. It buys from the vault at NAV and sells at NAV+1‑3%, so it earns a spread. Everything it earns above its working inventory is sent to Backing, i.e. to holders' floor. Those transfers show as 💝 in the live feed.

7. Backing v2: the real short on Robinhood Lighter

Robinhood Lighter is a perp exchange that runs as its own rollup on Robinhood Chain. Its bridge lets any address, including a contract, open an account by depositing USDG. LighterBacking is a contract that did exactly that. It owns Lighter account 23662 and is short PONS there at 1.5×.

ETH fees ──► LighterBacking.fund() : ETH → USDG → deposit to the contract's Lighter account keeper (trading key) : sells PONS on the order book until the short = 1.5× the collateral holder ──► __SYM__ burn → redeem() : L1 reduce-only close of your share + withdrawal, paid to the contract settle() : pays your claim in USDG when Lighter delivers it (≈1–7 min)

Why it's trustless. Lighter only ever pays withdrawals to the account's owner, which is the contract. Moving funds to another Lighter account needs the owner's private key, and a contract has none. The keeper holds a trading key that can open and close positions and nothing else. Worst case is a bad trade; theft is not possible.

Why the keeper is needed. Lighter's on-chain entry point can only place reduce-only orders (close, never open). So the contract registers one trading key once, and the keeper uses it to open the short. Closing does not need the key: redeem() sends a reduce-only close straight from the contract, so holders can always exit even if every keeper disappears.

Where v2's money comes from. ETH sent to the contract by anyone; the peg bot's profit; and FeeFeeder2, a liquidity position in the __SYM__/ETH pool that anyone can join. Depositors keep their principal and can withdraw it at any time. Every fee that liquidity earns goes to LighterBacking (ETH) and to burn (__SYM__); none of it goes to depositors. That is the "boost the short" button on the vault page.

v1 and v2 together. Same fees, same redeem button. v1 settles instantly on-chain and cannot be liquidated; v2 is the literal position on the real market and can (liquidation price is shown live). Holding __SYM__ is holding a slice of both.

9. What it is not

  • v1 is not a borrow. No PONS is sold in the vault. v2 does sell PONS on Lighter's order book, in the contract's own account.
  • The vault (v1) is 1× and pooled: payoff depends on the balance between sides (section 3). v2 is 1.5× on a real order book and has a liquidation price, shown live.
  • The floor is small early. It's a share of fees, not of market cap. It only grows.
  • No audit yet. The code is short (~600 lines) and public. Read it.

10. Verify it yourself, without our website

Every contract is verified on Blockscout. Open a contract → Read/Write contract → connect a wallet.

  • Vault: sharePriceWei(1) = sPONS NAV · shortBal()/longBal() · epochCount() = rebalances · owner() = 0x0
  • Backing: sponsBacking() = sPONS held · floorWeiPerToken() = what 1 __SYM__ redeems for · totalEthSwapped() + totalEthCommitted() = fee ETH converted
  • Go long: vault commitMint(0) with ETH → 2 min → claim(id, claimableEpoch(id)). Go short: commitMint(1). Exit: commitBurn(side, shares).
  • Redeem: __SYM__ redeem(amount).
  • v2 position, straight from the exchange: api.rh.lighter.xyz/api/v1/account?by=index&value=23662. On-chain mirror: LighterBacking baseTicks() (short size ×10), equityUnits() (USDG ×1e6), accountIndex().
  • Boost: FeeFeeder2 add(punzAmount) with ETH → withdraw(shares) any time; totalEthFed() = fees sent to the short.
