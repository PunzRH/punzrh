If the short is real and held by the contract, they agree. Nothing on this page comes from our server.

who controls the account

Can anyone withdraw to another address?No. Lighter's bridge pays withdrawals only to the account's L1 owner. L2 transfers need the owner's L1 private key; a contract has none.
Trading keyOne API key registered once (registerKey, one-shot). It can open/close positions and nothing else.
How a holder exitsredeem(amount) burns __SYM__ and sends an L1 reduce-only close for that share, no key needed. Paid in USDG by settle().
