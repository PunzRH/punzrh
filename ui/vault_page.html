__SYM__ vault — go long, go short, redeem

One pool of ETH, a long side and a short side. Every minute it reads PONS's price from PONS's own Uniswap pool; the losing side pays the winning side out of its own balance. sPONS = the short (what backs __SYM__), lPONS = the long. Everything here is a direct call to public contracts from your wallet; nobody is in the middle. Mints and burns settle at NAV after a full 2-minute price window so the oracle can't be front-run. Not advice. Both sides can lose.

Don't trust this page? You don't have to.

Connecting only reads your address. Minting is a plain ETH send to the vault; nothing here ever asks for a token approval except burning your own lPONS/sPONS. Every contract is verified on Blockscout, so you can do all of this there instead, on an explorer you already know: vault: write contract · PUNZ: redeem · read the source. Go long = commitMint(0) with ETH, then claim(id, claimableEpoch(id)) ~2 min later. This page is a single HTML file with no backend; view-source it.

Feed the Lighter short (v2) — keep your capital

Add ETH + __SYM__ as liquidity in the __SYM__/ETH pool through the FeeFeeder2 contract. You get shares and can withdraw your principal any time. You earn nothing from it: every fee the position collects is sent to LighterBacking (→ USDG → PONS short on Robinhood Lighter) and the __SYM__ side is burned. You must add BOTH sides (roughly equal value of ETH and __SYM__); leftovers are refunded in the same tx.
